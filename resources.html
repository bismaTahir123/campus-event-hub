Event Rules

Failure to comply with these rules may result in the cancellation of your event or loss of future booking privileges.

1. Booking & Scheduling

  • Advance Notice: Submit requests at least 5 working days prior to the event.
  • Cancellations: Notify the Events Office at least 48 hours in advance.
  • Time Limits: All events must conclude by 10:00 PM.

2. Safety & Security

  • Capacity limit: Attendees must not exceed the posted fire code capacity.
  • Emergency Exits: Fire lanes and exits must remain clear at all times.
  • Prohibited Items: No open flames,fireworks, or smoke machines indoors.

3. Venue Care

  • Cleanup: Organizers must leave the venue clean and place all trash in bins.
  • Decorations: No nails, screws, or strong adhesives on walls. Use painter's tape only.
  • Damages: The organizing group is financially responsible for any damages.

FAQs

How long does it take to get a booking approved?

Approvals typically take 2-3 business days. Make sure you submit your request at least 5 working days before your event to ensure enough time for processing.

What happens if I need to cancel?

You must email the Events Office at least 48 hours before your scheduled start time. Frequent last-minute cancellations may result in your student organization losing booking privileges for the semester.

Is tech support provided?

Basic tech support is provided for the Main Auditorium and Hall B-204 if requested in advance. For other rooms, we recommend testing your equipment beforehand or bringing your own portable speakers/projectors.
